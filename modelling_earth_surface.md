# Modeling Earth's Surface

### Spere
- We can assume Earth is a perfect spere with a radius of approximately 6371km
  
### Speriod (Ellipsoid)

- The true shape of the Earth is closer to an ellipsoid
- Defined by semi-major and semi-minor axis
- The most accurate and widely used ellipsoid is the WGS84 ellipsoid which as the following parameters
  - Semi-major axis ~ 6378.137 km
  - Semi-minor axis 6356.752 km
